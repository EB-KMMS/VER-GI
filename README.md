  <summary>Puzzle 3: Examine the foregut (3D model) </summary>
    Once you've had a look at the 3D model, you'll notice various letters on different parts of each organ. Find the tail of the pancreas first, then the caudate lobe of the liver, then pylorus of stomach, and finally the diaphragmatic surface of the spleen. Put the letters you found at each of those together and you should get a word.

  <summary>Puzzle 11: Find the patterns (Connections) </summary>
   The four categories are: Coeliac trunk territory, SMA territory, IMA territory, secondary retroperitoneal structures. Feel free to look terms up if you haven't heard them before. Remember you need to then take each item in the purple category, in alphabetical order and use their first letter (e.g. DUODENUM-BULB = "D") to form the password. IMPORTANT: the first letters do not form an actual word.
